---
name: Audit assurance boundaries
description: Why the agency intake must distinguish evidence collection from calibrated financial forecasts.
---

The full-audit intake's heatmap represents evidence readiness, not financial risk or probability of loss. Unknowns must remain explicit rather than silently turning into neutral assumptions.

**Why:** The existing screening assessment uses qualitative heuristics and assumed margin-erosion bands. Reviewing that logic did not establish empirical calibration. Collecting a more detailed questionnaire does not itself validate those predictions.

**How to apply:** Keep screening verdicts, collected agency evidence, calculated financial scenarios and uncertainty distinct. Do not claim that the intake generates an accurate forecast until a cost/revenue scenario model and historical validation have actually been implemented.
